Our history

BUILT ONE PROP AT A TIME

Kentucky Top Props was founded in Lexington in the late 1990s by a pair of set decorators who were tired of driving to Nashville or Atlanta every time a production needed something extraordinary.

Their solution: build the prop house they wished existed. What started as a few dozen pieces of period furniture and a couple of vintage vehicles has grown into one of the most comprehensive inventories in the mid-South — over 3,000 items spanning every era and aesthetic.

Over the years, we've served Hallmark Channel productions, regional theater companies, luxury real estate firms, and couples who wanted a 1965 T-Bird at their wedding. We treat every rental — whether it's a $40 side table or a $3,500 classic car — with the same care.

"We built the prop house we always wished existed. Everything we do starts with that."
— Founders, Kentucky Top Props

We're a small, passionate team based out of Lexington, and we genuinely love what we do. If you're looking for something specific, just ask — if we don't have it, we probably know where to find it.
